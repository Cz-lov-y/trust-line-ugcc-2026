Final Recommendation: BRT

BRT now. Preserve feasible future LRT options.

Choose BRT for the full Finch East corridor under the declared baseline.
CAD350M scenario cost = 2.10% of the capital ceiling; equal and capital-efficiency profiles support BRT. The cost point and delivery remain assumptions.

LRT-readiness: assess feasible corridor/structure clearances, coordinate utilities and retain useful as-built information. Reconsider rail when observed demand and operating constraints justify it.
Future conversion needs separate design, approvals, fleet/MSF, funding and works. Readiness and conversion costs are unestimated; no seamless-upgrade promise.

One technology for the current project. Service-priority weights can favour LRT; final engineering and AI compliance are not certified.

Source: preserved v0.3 model / v0.4 milestones; future-option measures are team proposals. (UTSRI & NRCI, 2026, pp.10–11).

S06 | TRUST-Line | AI assistance disclosed; scenario estimates and limits stated | 11

### 现有讲稿

Our final recommendation to LARPERS is BRT for the entire Finch East corridor. Under the assumed three-hundred-and-fifty-million-dollar cost point, it uses about two point one percent of the budget ceiling; that arithmetic is not a verified fiscal surplus. It wins our unchanged equal baseline and capital-efficiency priority, while LRT remains the service-priority alternative.

We propose protecting feasible future rail options through corridor and structure checks, coordinated utility siting and useful as-built records. Future conversion would need separate engineering, approvals, rail vehicles, maintenance arrangements, works and funding. It is not a seamless or free upgrade, and we have not costed it.

Choose BRT now under the priorities we have stated. Make its delivery assumptions accountable, and preserve tomorrow's options where evidence and design support them.
