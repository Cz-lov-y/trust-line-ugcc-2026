For each CT, its allocation factor is the area inside the catchment divided by its whole cartographic polygon area. Both population and low-income counts are multiplied by that factor; the proxy is the ratio of the summed allocated counts, not an average of tract percentages. Calculation uses EPSG:2952. Uniform within-CT population and low-income density are assumptions. These are spatial estimates, not observed corridor counts.

Official Census Profile characteristics: 1 (total population), 326 (LIM-AT low-income count) and 321 (LIM-applicable private-household population). Income reference year: 2020; Census population/geography: 2021. The numerator excludes collective-household residents from its income concept, while the requested denominator includes all residents. The custom share must not be called official LIM prevalence. Higher share is the user's chosen priority direction and measures local concentration, not predicted benefit distribution.
