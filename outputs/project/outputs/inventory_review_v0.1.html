Review findings

issuefindingaction
Raw-value availabilityThe 13-page attachment does not publish the unscaled 24-corridor indicator matrix. All 42 candidate raw columns remain blank. This is an evidence inventory, not a complete modelling dataset.Acquire the underlying model outputs or reconstruct comparable measures with documented methods.
Cost contaminationTen general measures are Cost-scaled before standardization. Multiplying z-scores by relative cost cannot recover raw benefits.Retain the benefit concepts but exclude the published cost-scaled versions from Part 1.
Construction lengthID 3 is 3 km with footnote 6, reduced for existing infrastructure. IDs 11 and 12 retain the original lengths pending geographic verification.Do not interpret the master length as verified full alignment length. Keep length_basis.
Source anomalyID 16 Relative Cost is printed as 87. No silent correction was made.Cost is excluded from the raw master and preserved as a documented source issue.
Baseline dependencyEight alternatives depend on other evaluated corridors. Transitive dependencies are retained.Resolve standalone versus extension benefit comparability before modelling.
Forecast horizonSeveral indicators refer to 2051, whereas the case requires operation by 2035.Keep original years; do not relabel forecasts.
Equity definitionsMain report p.16 identifies LIM-AT and Census visible-minority concept; p.17 recent immigrants includes 2016-2021 arrivals and non-permanent residents; p.18 long commute is one hour or more.Use the actual source populations. Preserve differences between household and person measures.
Equity ratiosTable 8 contains standardized scores, not the original Palma ratios. The complete transformation formula is not supplied.Do not apply a threshold of 1 to standardized scores or infer group counts.
Maps and route dataMain report p.21 uses mapped ridership/speed bands. External TTC annual route boardings are not equivalent to segment demand in Fall 2022.No midpoint substitutions or whole-route totals were assigned to candidate segments.
Geometry and facilitiesEndpoints alone do not specify an exact alignment or future station set. Supplementary indicators need catchment definitions.Record line geometry, stop set, buffer union, source geography and reference date before spatial aggregation.
Principle assignmentThe candidate longlist uses the requested user-facing principles; the City RTEF category is retained separately.Do not present the candidate grouping as an unchanged City hierarchy.
Pending admissionAll 42 candidate concepts remain pending evidence. A longlist is not a selection of 42 model criteria.Later review should address redundancy, coverage and policy meaning; no weighting performed.
