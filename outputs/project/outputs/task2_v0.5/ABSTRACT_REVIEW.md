TRUST-Line: BRT for Finch East, with Future LRT Options

TRUST-Line recommends bus rapid transit (BRT) for the 7.101 km Finch East corridor from Yonge Street to Victoria Park Avenue, selected using a cost-independent, tiered first-stage model. Subway is excluded from the conservative base case because a credible nine-year delivery and depot strategy has not been demonstrated. We computationally compare BRT and light rail transit using capital effectiveness, relative ridership and travel-time savings. Explicit scenario inputs are CAD350 million versus CAD950 million, ridership indices of 1.0 versus 1.2, and one-way savings of 2.5 versus 3.5 minutes. BRT leads under equal and capital-efficiency priorities; service-benefit priorities favour LRT. Reference-project cost proportions and four milestones make assumptions transparent (Metrolinx, 2020; Toronto Transit Commission, 2026). We recommend assessing feasible corridor and utility reservations for possible future LRT conversion, which would require separate engineering, funding and approval. Key limitations are transferred costs, assumed demand and time savings, policy-dependent results, and delivery risks. The analysis does not establish verified total costs, guaranteed 2035 opening or seamless conversion.
