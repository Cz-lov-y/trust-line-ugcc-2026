CONDITIONAL PRIORITY
Vary the level of priority depending on the streetcar’s headway or schedule adherence
• Early streetcars need to slow down, regardless of red lights
• Late streetcars need to catch up
• Schedule is based on above-average travel time.
• Speeding up early streetcars makes no difference to schedule

TAKEAWAYS
• Toronto has a strong TSP system, but it is
constrained by policy and intersection layouts.
•Tips to improve TSP:
•  Trial pedestrian-friendly multi-stage crossing timings
•  Explore opportunities to eliminate near-side stops
•  Introduce phase rotation and/or insertion (already
underway)
•  Use green compensation as standard practice
•  Continue rollout of conditional TSP – make sure
thresholds are aggressive.
